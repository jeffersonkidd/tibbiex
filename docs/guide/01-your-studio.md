# 1 · Your Studio

## Why

The Studio is the back office of your site: the tools for changing it and for
sharing it. The public never sees it. You'll use it every time you change your
picture or put your link somewhere new.

## Do it

1. Go to **tibbiex.studio/dashboard**. Bookmark it, on your phone too. (The
   old address, tibbiex.studio/studio, still brings you here.)
2. You'll see an **All access** pass hanging on a red lanyard. Type your
   **Studio passcode** on it and tap **Let me in**. The pass gets stamped
   **On stage** and the back office opens.
3. You'll see a small message at the top: _"This browser is now left out of
   the site's analytics."_ That's on purpose (see **The habit** below).
4. The strip under **Back office** switches tools. Right now there are three:
   - **Link Maker** — makes the link you paste into each profile (lesson 2).
   - **Avatar** — changes the face on your profile card (lesson 3).
   - **Products** — runs your shop (lesson 4).
5. **View site** in the top corner opens the public page, so you can check a
   change.

Each device asks for the passcode once. After that, the address walks you
straight in. You can bookmark a single tool, too:
**tibbiex.studio/dashboard#avatar** opens straight onto Avatar.

## Check it worked

The header says **Tibbie X Studio**, and the module strip shows Link Maker,
Avatar and Products.

## If something's off

- **The pass shakes and says _"That passcode isn't right."_** Check the
  spelling and try again. If it still won't take it, ask J.F. Don't guess
  repeatedly.
- **_"Too many tries from this connection."_** The site stops anyone who
  tries a lot of passcodes in a row, to keep guessers out. Wait ten minutes
  and try again.
- **_"The studio passcode changed. Type the new one."_** Someone changed the
  passcode, so this device has to be let in again. Ask J.F. for the new one.
- **Using a phone or computer that isn't yours?** Tap **Lock** in the top
  corner when you're done. It makes that device forget the passcode, so the
  next person gets the door, not your back office.
- **Keep the passcode to yourself** and to whoever posts for you. It's what
  stops a stranger changing your face or your shop.

## The habit

**Open the Studio once on every phone and computer you post from.** Getting in
leaves that browser out of the site's numbers, so your own visits (checking
the page, testing a link) don't get counted as fans. If the numbers are going
to tell you where fans come from, they have to be fans only.
