# 2 · Every post gets its own link

## Why

If you paste plain `tibbiex.studio` everywhere, every visit looks the same, and
you'll never know whether fans came from Instagram, TikTok or a flyer. Link
Maker gives each place its own version of your address. It still opens the
same page, but now the site knows where the visitor came from.

## Do it

Open **Studio → Link Maker**. Three steps, top to bottom:

1. **Where's it going?** Tap the profile: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook,
   Patreon, Threads, Bluesky, X or Email. For anywhere else (Linktree,
   Bandcamp, a poster) tap **Elsewhere** and type the name of the place.
2. **Where on the profile?** **Bio** for the link that stays put. **Story**,
   **Post** or **DM** for a link you drop somewhere for a day. **Other** lets
   you name it yourself ("pinned comment", "tour poster").
3. **Copy link.** The ticket stamps **COPIED**.

Then paste it. The card under the ticket shows the way there, menu by menu.
For Instagram it's **Profile → Edit profile → Links → Add external link →
Paste**. When it's in, tap **It's on my Instagram**.

Do the **Bio** link for every profile once. The **Profiles wired** list at the
bottom keeps score (9 in all).

## Check it worked

- The ticket shows your link with three coloured parts. Point at one to see
  what it means: **source** (which app), **medium** (what kind of link) and
  **campaign** (where on the profile). For an Instagram bio, it reads
  `tibbiex.studio/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio`.
- Tap the link on your profile. It should open your site as normal.
- The profile shows **Wired** in the list at the bottom.

## If something's off

- **Bluesky and Email have no link box.** Paste the link into the text itself
  (your Bluesky description, your email signature) on a line of its own. The
  Studio reminds you.
- **The app's menus don't match the steps.** Apps move their menus around.
  Find the link field, paste, and tell J.F. so the steps get fixed.
- **Making a Story link clears the profile's tick.** The tick tracks the last
  link you copied for that profile. Your bio link is still there and still
  working. To get the tick back, choose **Bio**, tap **Copy link**, then tap
  **It's on my …** again. You don't need to paste it again.
- **Ticks gone after clearing your browser?** They're kept in this browser
  only. The links themselves don't change, so a tick is a reminder, not a
  record.
- **Copy didn't work.** The Studio highlights the link instead. Copy it the
  normal way (long-press → Copy).

On a computer, keys **1–0** pick a profile and **C** copies.

## The habit

**Never paste the bare address.** Every time your link goes somewhere new,
make it in Link Maker first: a story, a post, a DM to a venue, a QR code on a
poster. It takes ten seconds. When the numbers come in (lesson 4), each of
those places will show up as its own row, so you'll know which post sent
people, not just which app.

A second habit comes with it: **one place, one link.** If the Instagram bio and
an Instagram story share a link, you can't tell them apart later. That's why
**Bio** and **Story** are separate choices.
