# 3 · Change your face

## Why

The picture on your profile card is the first thing people see on your site.
Avatar lets you swap it yourself, any time, without waiting on anyone.

## Do it

Open **Studio → Avatar**.

1. Drop, paste or pick a photo. JPG and PNG are the safest.
2. Frame it in the ring: **drag** to move it, and **slide or scroll** to zoom.
   On a computer, the arrow keys move it and plus / minus zoom.
3. Type your **Studio passcode**. Each device asks for it once, *"so a stranger
   can't change your face."*
4. Tap **Make it live**.

You'll see *"New face is live. The site catches up within a minute."*

To undo, tap **Back to the original**. That brings back the portrait the site
shipped with.

## Check it worked

Wait a minute, then tap **View site**. The profile card shows the new face.
The Studio also shows what's live now. If you've never changed it, that's
**Live · the original portrait**.

## If something's off

- **"That isn't an image" / "Couldn't open that image."** Save it as a JPG or
  PNG and try again. Screenshots work.
- **The site still shows the old face.** Give it a minute, then reload the
  page.
- **Wrong passcode.** Ask J.F. Don't guess repeatedly.

## The habit
![alt text](https://file%2B.vscode-resource.vscode-cdn.net/Users/jeffersonkidd/Desktop/jkidd-ds/packages/blocks/src/assets/fronts/western.jpg?version%3D1792049368249)
**One face, everywhere, changed on purpose.** Fans recognize you by your
picture before they read your name. When you change the face on the site,
change it on every profile the same day, so someone who taps through from
Instagram lands on the same person they just saw. Lesson 2's **Profiles
wired** list doubles as your checklist of profiles to update.

Change it for a reason: a new release, a new era, a tour. A face that changes
every week doesn't become recognizable.
